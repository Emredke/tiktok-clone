# Velo sound library

The nine sounds in server/sounds.js are original synthesized compositions and effects created for Velo. They contain no samples or third-party recordings. Velo dedicates these compositions and recordings to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . You can use them in published videos, including commercial videos. Attribution is optional; the app displays the sound title and license for transparency.

User-uploaded or recorded voiceovers remain user content. The sound-library dedication does not apply to user voiceovers or imported video footage.
